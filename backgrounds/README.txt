Focus Board backgrounds
=======================

Each theme has its own folder here. Put up to two images in it:

  page.jpg    -> background of the whole app
  plane.jpg   -> background of the priority plane

Any of .jpg .jpeg .png .webp .gif .avif works (animated GIFs animate).
Images in "default" are used by every theme that has no image of its own.

The app picks up new or replaced files when its window regains focus
(or press Reload in the Backgrounds panel, the picture icon in the top bar).
Adjust how faded each image is from that same panel.
